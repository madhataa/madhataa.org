Clock Changes

Daylight-saving time is not the same everywhere. This page shows exactly when each listed place changes between summer time and standard time, and what happens to the meeting time there.

How to read this

Summer time starts: the clock moves forward, so the UTC offset gets larger.

Standard time starts: the clock moves back, so the UTC offset gets smaller.

The cards below cover the next six months from today. Places that do not change their clocks in that period simply do not appear.
